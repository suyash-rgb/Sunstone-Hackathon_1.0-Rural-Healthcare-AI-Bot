# Unified Emergency Healthcare Facility Search Engine Walkthrough

We have implemented the **Unified Emergency Search Engine** (`GET /api/v1/emergency/facilities/nearby`) that concurrently queries both the local PostgreSQL database (containing 192,905 verified public health facilities) and external POI services (OlaMaps / OpenStreetMap), merging and sorting all facilities strictly by **distance** with a **Top 5 Government Facility Guarantee**.

---

Key Features & Logic

1. Guaranteed Government Representation in Top 5
- After sorting all combined results by distance ascending, the system checks if at least one government health facility is present in the **Top 5 results**.
- If no government facility is present in the initial top 5 (e.g. urban areas surrounded by private clinics), the system automatically finds the **nearest verified Government DB facility** and promotes it to **position #5**.
- This ensures rural citizens using the app always see at least one verified public healthcare option directly on their main screen without having to scroll deep down.

2. Resiliency & Graceful Degradation
- Implemented `asyncio.gather(return_exceptions=True)` to execute queries in parallel.
- If external APIs fail or time out, the backend gracefully degrades to returning only verified Government DB facilities without throwing an HTTP 500 error.

3. Spatial Deduplication
- If an external POI is within 150 meters of a verified Government DB facility, the verified NIN record takes precedence and the duplicate external entry is filtered out.
