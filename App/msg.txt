Add Voice Recording functionality and audio player styling

- Installed and integrated expo-audio for native voice recording and playback capabilities.
- Added a push-to-talk microphone button that pulses red while recording, closely mimicking WhatsApp's behavior.
- Extracted recording functionality into a separate `src/RecordingBar.js` component to keep `App.js` clean and modular.
- Added a custom audio player UI (`AudioMessage`) in `App.js` with playback controls, duration, and a functional progress bar.
- Fixed audio player progression tracking and implemented manual replay support (preventing automatic loop).
- Updated audio player CSS in `src/styles.js` for better visual contrast and premium feel in light mode.
